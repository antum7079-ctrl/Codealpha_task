A comprehensive Supply Chain & Logistics Dashboard for Samsung, visualizing end-to-end operational metrics across suppliers, inventory, shipments, and customers.
This dashboard provides actionable insights into performance indicators such as total revenue, profit margin, order quantity, shipment delays, and customer distribution.
It integrates supplier lead times, inventory values by product, carrier delays, and customer revenue contributions — enabling data-driven decision-making and supply chain optimization.

Key Highlights:

📦 Supplier performance and average lead times

🏭 Inventory valuation by product line

🚚 Shipment quantity and carrier delay analysis

👥 Customer revenue segmentation

💰 Profit margin and perfect order rate tracking
